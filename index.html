How to play

  1. Choose 2–4 players and take turns rolling.
  2. Reach square 100 exactly to win. Overshooting keeps you in place.
  3. Land at a ladder's foot to climb up.
  4. Land on a snake's head and slide down to its tail.
  5. The first player to reach 100 wins!
